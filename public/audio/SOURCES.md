# Audio sources

The bundled clips are trimmed, mono, loudness-normalized derivatives of public-domain recordings from Wikimedia Commons.

- piano.mp3: “Für Elise,” composed by Ludwig van Beethoven and performed by V. Gao. [Source and CC0 dedication](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FurElise.ogg)
- drums.mp3: “Drums,” recorded by imakepitart. [Source and public-domain dedication](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drums.ogg)
- rain.mp3: “Rain,” recorded by ジダネ. [Source and public-domain dedication](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain.ogg)
- full-song.mp3: “Play House - FREE,” written and produced by Play House. Complete 4:41 track. [Source and CC0 license](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/play-house/single/play-house-free/)
